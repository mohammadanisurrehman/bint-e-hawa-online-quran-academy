About the academy

Founded to make Qur'an learning feel personal, not distant

Bint e Hawa Online Quran Academy was founded to give students — wherever they live — the same closeness to the Qur'an that a student would find sitting beside a teacher at home. Every class is one-on-one, live, and shaped around the student's own pace and level.

Classes are taught personally by the founder, trained in correct Tajweed and classical teaching methods, so every student gets focused, consistent attention from someone who knows their progress well.

  • Personal, one-on-one teaching, fluent in English and Urdu
  • Classes scheduled around your timezone, any day of the week
  • Regular progress updates shared with parents
